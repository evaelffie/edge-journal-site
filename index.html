Pro subscription

On iPhone, Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription paid through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. To get Pro back on a new phone, open the Pro page in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete my account.

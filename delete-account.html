EDGE JOURNAL

Delete your account

Edge Journal, by Nathan

In the app

1. Open Edge Journal and sign in.
2. Go to Settings.
3. Under Delete my account, type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Without the app

Email support@clockinsite.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Edge Journal account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything: your login, email and name, trades, missed trades, notes, day summaries, screenshots, trading accounts, trading model and AI Coach chats. Nothing is kept afterwards. A website membership is cancelled at the same time. If you pay through Apple, cancel the subscription in your iPhone settings as well.

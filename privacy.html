Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

What we keep

Your email address and name, the trades, missed trades, notes and day summaries you write, the screenshots you attach, your trading accounts and their rules, your trading model answers, and your AI Coach chats. We also count how many AI requests you use each month.

Why we keep it

Only to run the journal for you: to show your trades, work out your stats and progress, and, if you are on Pro, to let Claude read your screenshots and answer your questions. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Who else handles it

Supabase stores your journal and screenshots and handles sign-in. On Pro, the screenshots and journal details needed for each request are sent to Anthropic, which runs Claude, and are not used to train its models. Payments are handled by Apple (in the iPhone app, through RevenueCat) or Stripe (on the website). We never see your card details. On the free plan, Ask in Claude only copies a question for you to paste into your own Claude account.

Your data is private

Each member can only ever read their own journal. Screenshots are stored privately and are only shown to you through short-lived links.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings and tap Delete my account. Your journal, screenshots and login are removed straight away. If you pay through Apple, cancel the subscription in your iPhone settings as well.
